Historical scenario details

The future line follows the monthly closes of a past window with a similar total return, rescaled to the selected bearish, moderate, or bullish endpoint. Its turns and timing are illustrations, not predictions. The chart gives history its own width and uses a logarithmic price scale by default so longer horizons remain readable.
