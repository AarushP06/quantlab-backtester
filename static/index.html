Read the study with its limits

The universe contains 20 large caps selected today, creating upward selection bias in historical comparisons. Signals use information from the current bar and execute at the next bar’s open; adjusted historical prices are not executable quotes.

Research, not investment advice. Past performance does not predict future returns.

This site uses saved, precomputed results. Arbitrary date ranges require the local Flask app; no backtest runs in your browser.
